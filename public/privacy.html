Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 2, 2026

AI SEO Optimizer ("we", "us", or "our") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how merchant and store information is collected, used, and protected when you install and use the AI SEO Optimizer application (the "App") on Shopify.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect and access data that is strictly required to audit store SEO health, generate SEO metadata, and synchronize optimized content directly to your Shopify store:

  • Store Identification: Your store's .myshopify.com domain, store name, primary language, and country code to configure app settings.
  • Catalog Data: Product titles, descriptions, vendors, handles, product tags, collection titles, pages, and blog post content needed to generate accurate SEO titles, descriptions, and keywords.
  • Media & File Assets: Image URLs and existing ALT text for product images, collection banners, and store files to identify missing ALT tags and generate descriptive accessibility copy.
  • Shopify OAuth Access Tokens: Granted strictly through official Shopify Admin APIs with scoped permissions (write_products, write_files, write_content) to read unoptimized catalog items and save approved SEO updates.
End-Consumer Personal Data Protection:

AI SEO Optimizer DOES NOT collect, process, store, or sell any personal identifiable information (PII) belonging to your store's customers. We do not collect customer names, email addresses, physical addresses, credit cards, or order records.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected catalog and store information exclusively to:

  • Calculate your store's live 0–100 SEO health score across products, collections, pages, blogs, and images.
  • Generate search-engine-friendly SEO titles (under 50 characters) and click-worthy meta descriptions (under 150 characters).
  • Extract relevant target keywords and synchronize them to Shopify's standard seo.keywords metafield definition.
  • Auto-generate accessible, WCAG-compliant image ALT text using smart customizable templates.
  • Save approved metadata back to Shopify native SEO fields and theme fallback tags.
  • Authenticate your merchant session inside the Shopify Admin panel.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We treat your store data with absolute confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or trade your catalog or store information to any third parties.

  • AI Generation: When using AI generation features, catalog text is processed strictly in real-time through secure, enterprise-grade AI APIs. Your content is never used to train public machine learning models.
  • Infrastructure: Data is hosted on secure, isolated servers with encrypted communication over HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • No Advertising Trackers: We do not inject tracking pixels, advertising scripts, or analytics beacons into your merchant admin or online storefront.

4. Data Retention & Automatic Deletion

We retain your store settings and audit cache only for the duration that the application remains installed on your Shopify store.

  • Instant Deletion Upon Uninstall: When you uninstall the App from your Shopify store, Shopify triggers the mandatory app/uninstalled webhook. Upon receipt, your store access tokens, session keys, and cached audit records are automatically purged from our database.
  • Permanent Merchant Control: All SEO titles, meta descriptions, keywords, and ALT text already published to your products remain permanently saved in your Shopify store.

5. Mandatory Shopify & GDPR Compliance

AI SEO Optimizer strictly adheres to Shopify's API Terms and European GDPR / California CCPA regulations. We implement all required Shopify privacy compliance webhooks:

  • customers/data_request: Automatically processed to verify that no end-customer data is stored by the App.
  • customers/redact: Processed immediately to confirm zero customer personal data exists.
  • shop/redact: Automatically deletes all remaining store settings within 48 hours of an official store deletion.

6. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your store data:

  • All communications between Shopify, our application, and your browser are encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • Shopify access tokens are stored securely in restricted database storage with zero public exposure.
  • Regular automated dependency audits and security monitoring.
